# LinkedIn Project Showcase Skill

This skill helps developers and hackathon participants translate complex technical projects into compelling, professional LinkedIn launch assets and career profile updates.

## Workflow

1. **Extract Technical Differentiators**:
   - What problem does the project solve?
   - What is the non-trivial engineering challenge (e.g., Human-in-the-Loop safety, multimodal OCR, low-literacy UX)?
   - What tech stack was leveraged (Gemini API, Streamlit, Pydantic, Python)?

2. **Structure the Launch Post**:
   - **Hook**: Stop the scroll with a real-world human challenge (e.g., medical handwriting in rural clinics).
   - **The Problem**: Why existing generic solutions fail (e.g. AI hallucinations in clinical dosage).
   - **The Core Innovation**: How the app works (Scan ➔ Read ➔ Verify ➔ Explain ➔ Remind).
   - **Key Safety Invariants**: Show engineering maturity (Human confirmation gates, zero hallucinations).
   - **Tech Stack & Hackathon Tags**: Mention Google Gemini, Streamlit, Python, and event organizers.
   - **Call-to-Action**: Invite feedback, review, or demo testing.

3. **Map Professional Skills for LinkedIn**:
   - Provide the exact list of standardized LinkedIn technical and domain skills to add to a user's LinkedIn profile.
