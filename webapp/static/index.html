Explore health information, discuss symptoms and plausible causes, understand medicines, and prepare questions for a clinician. Saathi can offer a tentative assessment, explain what remains uncertain, and help you decide on a next step.

USEFUL CONTEXT

Possible causes, not certainty

Saathi can compare plausible causes from what you report, ask focused questions, and explain warning signs. It cannot confirm a diagnosis or recommend changing your prescribed dose.
