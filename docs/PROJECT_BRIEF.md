# Sehat Saathi — project brief

## What is it?

Sehat Saathi is a health-information companion for people who want help keeping medicine instructions and everyday health notes organized. Its name means “health companion” in Hindi.

## Who is it for?

The idea is to support patients, older adults, and family caregivers—especially people who find prescriptions or digital health tools difficult to follow. The current interface is mainly in English, with some India-focused Hindi content.

## What can it do?

- Read a prescription image and show the text it could identify for a person to review.
- Create medicine reminders only after the person checks and confirms the details.
- Record health readings, wellbeing check-ins, recovery notes, and medicine concerns.
- Answer some general questions about medicine instructions, with limits against diagnosis.
- Show a small demo directory of nearby care services for selected places around Rajasthan and the NCR region.
- Display India-focused emergency and support contacts.

## Why does it exist?

Its goal is to make health instructions easier to understand and organize, and to help people prepare for conversations with doctors, pharmacists, and caregivers. It is designed to assist people—not make care decisions for them.

## What should users know?

This is a **local research demo**, not an approved health service. It has not been validated for clinical decisions. It does not provide nationwide coverage in India or the United States. It has no sign-in or secure separation between people, and some records are kept in local project files. Use only synthetic example information; do not enter real patient data. Always confirm medical instructions with a doctor or pharmacist.

## Longer-term aim

The longer-term aim is to explore a clear, accessible way for people and caregivers to keep track of care information. Any real-world pilot would need clinical review, privacy and security controls, official regional information, accessibility and language testing, and approval from the organizations responsible for the service.
